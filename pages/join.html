NSS is a voluntary student programme focused on community service and learning through service. At Raha College, the NSS unit enrols eligible degree-class students through the Programme Officer.

  • NSS motto: NOT ME BUT YOU
  • Community service is the central activity.
  • Enrollment is handled through the NSS Programme Officer.
  • Participation involves service activities, orientation and special-camp/community work.
