About NSS

National Service Scheme

NSS is a Central Sector Scheme of the Government of India that gives student youth opportunities to learn through voluntary community service.

What does NSS stand for, and when was it formally launched?

NSS stands for National Service Scheme. It was formally launched on 24 September 1969to develop the personality and character of student youth through community service. Its motto is “NOT ME BUT YOU”.

1969National Service Scheme launched nationally

Raha College NSS

Institutional records state that the Raha College NSS unit was set up in 1978 under Gauhati University. The unit has conducted community-oriented activities including cleanliness drives, plantation, blood donation, awareness work and disaster-related service.

1978NSS unit at Raha College documented as established

Raha College

A place to learn & serve

Raha College was established on 28 July 1964 and is permanently affiliated to Gauhati University.

Raha College, Nagaon

Raha College began its journey in 1964 through the efforts of people from the greater Raha area. The college serves students from Raha and adjoining areas and offers higher education in Arts, Science and Commerce.

The official college site describes a campus with classrooms, departments, library, computer facilities, sports facilities, auditorium and other student-support infrastructure.
